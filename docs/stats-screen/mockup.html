The backlog screen

The stats screen for a media items category, in two independent halves: what you have finished, as a history by year, and what you have not, taken apart by status, importance and platform. Every figure is computed live from a generated item set, so the filters really do recompute the page.

Media items stats

The category switch above is a mockup control and is not part of the screen — the real screen only ever shows the category the user came from. Everything inside the frame is: the two filters, the tooltips on the year bars, and the empty states when a filter leaves nothing behind. The written specification is in the README.md beside this file, in docs/stats-screen/.
